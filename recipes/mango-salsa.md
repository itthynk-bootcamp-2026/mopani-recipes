# Mango salsa

**Serves:** 4 · **Time:** 15 minutes · **Contributed by:** @mopani-maintainers

## Ingredients

- 2 ripe mangoes, diced
- 1 small red onion, finely chopped
- Juice of 1 lemon
- A handful of coriander

## Method

1. Mix everything in a bowl.
2. Season with salt and rest for 10 minutes before serving.
